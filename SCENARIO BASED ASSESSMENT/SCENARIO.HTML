How to register

  1. Confirm your department and current semester with your advisor.
  2. Check that prerequisite courses from the previous semester are cleared.
  3. Select one or more courses below, keeping total credits within the 22-credit cap.
  4. Fill in the registration form with your official register number and university email.
  5. Review the generated summary carefully before your advisor countersigns it.
